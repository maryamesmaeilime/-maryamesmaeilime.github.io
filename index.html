About Me

My research focuses on rock-mass characterization and ground-deformation hazard assessment using remote sensing, UAV surveys, LiDAR, image processing, and 2D/3D point-cloud analysis.

Research Interests

  • Engineering geology and natural hazards
  • Rock-fracture characterization
  • LiDAR and UAV remote sensing
  • 2D/3D image and point-cloud processing
  • Hyperspectral remote sensing
  • Fractal analysis
